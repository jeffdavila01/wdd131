KEEP IT ROAD-READY

Maintenance Guide

Simple inspection habits can help you identify problems before they become bigger concerns.

01

Before Every Ride

Check the tires, brakes, lights, mirrors, and general condition of the motorcycle before riding.

02

Engine Oil

Check the oil level according to the motorcycle manufacturer's instructions and follow the recommended service interval.

03

Chain Care

Keep the drive chain clean, properly lubricated, and adjusted according to manufacturer specifications.

04

Brake Inspection

Pay attention to unusual noises, reduced braking performance, or visible signs of wear.

05

Tire Condition

Check pressure, tread condition, and visible damage. Use the recommended tire pressure for your motorcycle.

06

Electrical System

Confirm that headlights, brake lights, indicators, and other important electrical components operate correctly.

RIDE RESPONSIBLY

Safety Reminders

Helmet

Wear an appropriate helmet that fits correctly and is suitable for motorcycle riding.

Protective Gear

Consider gloves, protective clothing, and proper footwear appropriate for the ride.

Road Awareness

Stay alert, maintain a safe following distance, and adjust your riding to traffic and road conditions.
