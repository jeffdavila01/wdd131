Motorcycle Parts

ESSENTIAL COMPONENTS

Know Your Motorcycle

Regular inspection becomes easier when you understand the basic purpose of each major component.

01

Engine Components

The engine converts fuel and air into mechanical power that drives the motorcycle.

Why it matters

Proper oil levels, regular servicing, and attention to unusual sounds or performance changes can help maintain engine reliability.

02

Brake System

The brake system allows the rider to reduce speed and stop safely.

Why it matters

Brake pads, discs, fluid, and other components should be inspected regularly for wear or damage.

03

Tires

Tires provide contact between the motorcycle and road surface and should be checked regularly.

Why it matters

Proper tire pressure and sufficient tread help support handling, braking, and road contact.

04

Chain & Sprockets

The chain transfers power from the engine to the rear wheel.

Why it matters

Cleaning, lubrication, proper tension, and inspection help reduce unnecessary chain wear.

05

Battery

The battery supplies electrical power for starting and other motorcycle systems.

Why it matters

Battery terminals and electrical connections should be kept clean and checked when starting problems occur.

06

Lights

Headlights, brake lights, and turn signals help riders see and communicate with other road users.

Why it matters

Working lights improve visibility and help other road users recognize your intentions.
